# True Cost Unlocked - Upcoming Features Roadmap

The following AI-powered features were brainstormed and approved for future implementation:

## 1. Jargon Translator (Plain English Glossary)
Contracts are intentionally packed with confusing terms (like "Factor Rate", "Amortization", or "Indemnification"). 
**Implementation Idea:** The AI scans the document, extracts the 3 to 5 most confusing financial/legal words actually used in the text, and generates a small "Glossary" card in the dashboard with simple, one-sentence plain-English explanations for each.

## 2. Alternative Funding Strategies
Instead of just warning the user, the AI proactively suggests completely different, cheaper ways to get the money based on their specific situation.
**Implementation Idea:** Add a new section where the AI generates 1-2 alternative scenarios. *(Example: "Instead of taking this 60-month personal loan at a 42% markup, you could apply for a 0% APR introductory balance-transfer credit card.")*

## 3. Fair Market Benchmark
The AI evaluates the quoted interest rate and fees against current national market averages.
**Implementation Idea:** Create a comparison widget that tells the user exactly how much they are being overcharged compared to the rest of the market. *(Example: "This 8.99% rate is actually 2.5% higher than the current national average for personal loans").*
